Navigate to the "Add Result" section from the navbar. Enter your exam name, date, and individual subject marks. You can add multiple subjects at once and the system will automatically calculate your percentage and grade.

Your data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This means your results are only stored on your device and not sent to any server. Your data remains private and secure.

Grades are calculated based on your percentage: A (90-100%), B (80-89%), C (70-79%), D (60-69%), F (Below 60%). The system automatically assigns grades for each subject and calculates your overall GPA.
